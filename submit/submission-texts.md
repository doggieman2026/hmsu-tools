# CrazyGames 提出用テキスト

すべて英語で入力する。表紙は各ゲームの *-covers/ にある（landscape 1920x1080 / portrait 800x1200 / square 800x800）。
提出する順番：1. Twin Steps → 2. Time Flock → 3. Star Shift → 4. Maze Dash（Mandala by Numbers は出さない）

## 1. Twin Steps
- Category: Puzzle
- Tags: logic, brain, casual, mobile-friendly
- Controls: Arrow keys / WASD or swipe. Z = undo, R = restart, H = hint.
- Short description:
  One move, two boards. Guide both blobs onto their stars at the same time.
- Description:
  Twin Steps is a calm logic puzzle with a twist: every move pushes two pieces on two different boards. Walls stop one piece while the other keeps going. Use that to bring both blobs onto their stars at the very same moment.
  A new Daily puzzle every day, the same for everyone, with a shareable result. Plus 48 checked levels that introduce new rules as you go: Mirror boards flip left and right, and Ice makes pieces slide until they hit something. Solve each level within par for three stars, and use undo or a hint whenever you get stuck.

## 2. Time Flock
- Category: Shooting
- Tags: arcade, space, bullet-hell, roguelike
- Controls: Move with the mouse, drag on touch, or use arrow keys / WASD. Your ship fires on its own. P = pause.
- Short description:
  Time moves when you move. Every enemy you destroy joins your flock.
- Description:
  Time Flock is an arcade shooter where you control time by moving. Pause for a moment and the world slows down, so you can find a gap in the bullets before you go.
  Every enemy you destroy turns and joins your flock, firing alongside you in its own style. But your flock is part of your ship: if a bullet hits an ally, you lose a life. Grow a bigger swarm, take on tougher waves, and beat the boss every fifth wave.

## 3. Star Shift
- Category: Shooting
- Tags: arcade, space, roguelike, upgrade
- Controls: Move with the mouse, drag on touch, or use arrow keys / WASD. Shift = slow focus. P = pause.
- Short description:
  Survive the waves, pick a new power after each one, and beat the boss every fifth wave.
- Description:
  Star Shift is a neon wave shooter with roguelite upgrades. Your ship fires automatically, so all you have to do is dodge and steer.
  After each wave, pick one of three upgrades: rapid fire, spread shot, piercing rounds, orbiting drones, shields, critical hits and more. Combine them into a build that can survive five enemy types and an escalating boss fight every fifth wave.

## 4. Maze Dash
- Category: Puzzle
- Tags: maze, casual, time-attack, mobile-friendly
- Controls: Drag the ball along the path, or use arrow keys / WASD. R = restart, Esc = menu.
- Short description:
  Race the clock through 60 mazes. Faster runs earn more stars.
- Description:
  Maze Dash is a fast maze game for quick sessions. Trace your way from start to the star, and beat the clock to earn up to three stars on each level.
  60 levels grow from tiny warm-ups to sprawling labyrinths, with "lights out" stages where you can only see around yourself. A new Daily Challenge of five mazes waits every day.
